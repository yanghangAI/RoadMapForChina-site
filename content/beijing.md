# Beijing · 北京

**July 14 (arrive 12:10) – July 16 evening · 2.5 days**
**Group:** 6 people (5 Italians + 1 Chinese friend joining locally)
**Base:** Near 丽都饭店, Jiangtai Lu, Chaoyang District
**Transport:** Rental car (Chinese friend drives) · pick up at Daxing Airport

---

## ⚠️ Book Immediately

- **Forbidden City tickets** — max 40,000 visitors/day in July, sells out weeks ahead → [gugong.dpm.org.cn](https://gugong.dpm.org.cn)
- **Night train Beijing West → Xi'an (Jul 16)** — 6 people, book 2 soft sleeper compartments (4-berth) → [12306.cn](https://www.12306.cn)

---

## Rental Car Notes

- Only your Chinese friend can legally drive — foreign licenses are not valid in China
- Book a **6–7 seat MPV** (Buick GL8 or similar) at Daxing Airport on arrival
- Drop off near **Beijing West Station** before the Jul 16 night train

---

## Day 1 · July 14 — Arrival Afternoon

*Arrive Daxing 12:10 · ~4 usable hours after landing + drive*

- **~12:10** Land at Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX)
- **~2:00pm** Clear customs, collect luggage, pick up rental car
- **~3:30pm** Arrive Jiangtai Lu — drop bags, freshen up
- **4:00–6:00pm** [798 Art District](https://www.google.com/search?q=798+Art+District+Beijing&tbm=isch) 大山子艺术区 — 5min drive from home. Galleries, street art, industrial architecture, coffee. Easy intro to Beijing with no crowd pressure.
- **7:30pm** 🍽 Peking duck dinner — **Da Dong (大董)**, Gongti Beilu branch, 10min drive. Best introduction to Beijing food for the Italian guests.

---

## Day 2 · July 15 — Great Wall

*Full day · ~1.5h drive each way*

- **7:30am** Depart — drive to **Mutianyu Great Wall (慕田峪长城)**, ~75km north
- **9:00am** Arrive Mutianyu
- **9:00am–1:00pm** Great Wall — cable car up, walk the restored 3.5km section, **toboggan slide down**. Less crowded than Badaling, more scenic, original Ming Dynasty stonework.
- **1:30pm** Lunch at **Brickyard Restaurant** near Mutianyu — international-friendly menu, garden setting, popular with tour groups
- **3:30pm** Drive back south — stop at **Olympic Park (奥林匹克公园)** on the way: Bird's Nest + Water Cube from outside, 30min photo stop, no tickets needed
- **6:00pm** Back at Jiangtai Lu
- **7:30pm** 🍽 Dinner: **Haidilao hotpot** (海底捞), Sanlitun branch — great first hotpot experience, good service, manageable for first-timers

## Day 3 · July 16 — Imperial City + Night Train

*Morning + afternoon · depart ~8:30pm for Beijing West Station*

- **8:00am** Drive to Tiananmen area — park at Jingshan Park lot
- **8:30–10:00am** **Tiananmen Square (天安门广场)** — arrive early before the heat and peak crowds. Portrait of Mao, Monument to the People's Heroes, Gate of Heavenly Peace.
- **10:00am–1:00pm** **Forbidden City (故宫博物院)** — pre-booked tickets required. 3 hours covers the main south-to-north axis (Meridian Gate → Hall of Supreme Harmony → Palace of Heavenly Purity → Imperial Garden) plus one side courtyard.
- **1:30pm** Lunch: **Wangfujing (王府井)** pedestrian street — street snacks, egg waffles, scorpion-on-a-stick (optional for the brave)
- **3:00–5:00pm** **Nanluoguxiang hutongs (南锣鼓巷)** — historic alleyways, courtyard homes, small shops, local ice cream. Walk or take a rickshaw tour through the lanes.
- **6:00pm** Return to Jiangtai Lu — pack bags, load car
- **7:30pm** Drop rental car near Beijing West, get to **Beijing West Station (北京西站)**
- **~9:00pm** Board night train to Xi'an (Z-series sleeper)
- *Arrives Xi'an ~7:30am July 17*

## Practical Notes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Weather** | July in Beijing: 30–36°C, humid, occasional afternoon thunderstorms. Bring sunscreen and a light rain jacket. |
| **Cash** | WeChat Pay / Alipay dominate. Set up one Chinese friend's account for group payments. Some tourist spots accept cards. |
| **Language** | Your Chinese friend handles this. Outside tourist areas, almost no English. |
| **Great Wall crowds** | Arrive at Mutianyu by 9am to beat tour buses that arrive 10am–noon. |
| **Forbidden City** | No large bags allowed. Store luggage at the south gate cloakroom (¥10). Photography allowed in courtyards, not inside halls. |
| **Parking** | Central Beijing has paid parking lots near all major sights. Budget ¥30–50 per stop. |
