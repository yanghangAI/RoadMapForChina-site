# Packing List · China Trip July 2026

This 22-day trip crosses multiple climate zones: humid urban heat in Beijing and Xi'an (30–38°C), high-altitude lakeside (15°C), Qaidam desert basin (28°C), Gobi desert (42°C), and alpine grassland (10–22°C). Pack for all of them.

---

## Clothing

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Light down jacket or fleece | Qinghai Lake, Chaka, Qilian mornings are cold (8–15°C) |
| 3–4 lightweight shirts | Quick-dry preferred |
| 1 pair long trousers | Sun protection in desert; required in Mogao Caves |
| 2 pairs shorts | For hot days |
| Warm socks | For Qilian morning horseback ride |
| Comfortable walking shoes | For dunes, fort walls, cave paths |
| Sandals or flip-flops | Hotel evenings; salt lake wading (Chaka) |
| Old shoes you don't mind ruining | Salt brine at Chaka and Feicui Hu destroys leather |
| Light rain jacket | Beijing July has afternoon thunderstorms |
| Sun hat with full brim | Dunhuang sun is severe; Xi'an City Wall has no shade |
| Sunglasses | Essential — Chaka salt flat reflection is blinding |
| Light scarf or buff | Desert dust, sun protection on neck |

---

## Sun & Health

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Sunscreen SPF 50+ | Reapply every 2hrs in Dunhuang |
| Lip balm with SPF | Desert air is extremely dry |
| Moisturiser | Qaidam Basin humidity is near zero |
| Altitude sickness tablets (高原反应药) | Optional but useful for Day 1–2 at 3,200m |
| Paracetamol / ibuprofen | Headaches are common at altitude |
| Electrolyte sachets or sports drink tablets | Critical for Dunhuang heat days and Xi'an City Wall cycling |
| Insect repellent | Beijing hutongs and evening markets attract mosquitoes in July |
| Personal medications | Nearest major hospital is Xining on the northwest loop |
| Basic first aid: plasters, antiseptic | |

## Documents & Money

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Passport (originals) | Required for hotel check-in in China — every night |
| Passport copies (photo + digital) | Keep a photocopy separate from original; photo on phone as backup |
| Printed hotel bookings | Some rural hotels don't accept digital |
| Forbidden City booking confirmation | Print or screenshot — timed entry |
| Mogao Caves booking confirmation | Print or screenshot — needed at gate |
| Terracotta Warriors booking confirmation | Print or screenshot |
| Train tickets / 12306 confirmations | T231, 7006, D2685 — screenshot or print |
| Cash RMB (¥2,000–3,000 per person) | Rural areas often cash-only |
| Bank card (Visa/Mastercard) | Works at ATMs in cities; carry as backup |

> **Italian note:** Chinese ATMs in Beijing, Xi'an, Xining, Dunhuang, and Jiayuguan accept foreign Visa/Mastercard. In Chaka, Dachaidan, and Qilian, ATM availability is limited. Withdraw cash in Xining before the northwest loop.

## Tech & Navigation

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Phone charger + power bank | Long driving days with navigation drain battery fast |
| Car phone mount | For navigation — essential |
| Offline maps downloaded | Amap (高德地图) or Baidu Maps work in China; Google Maps does not |
| VPN installed and tested before entering China | Cannot be downloaded inside China |
| Camera / extra memory cards | Chaka and Danxia especially |
| Universal power adapter | China uses Type A and Type I — most European plugs need adapter |

## Night Train Essentials (T231 Beijing → Xi'an)

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Earplugs + sleep mask | Train corridors have noise and light all night |
| Slippers | For walking to the toilet car — don't go barefoot |
| Snacks and water | The dining car closes early; bring enough for the evening |
| Small towel | Sleeper compartments have no towels |
| Entertainment (phone/book) | No reliable WiFi on the train |
| Padlock or cable lock | Optional — soft sleeper doors lock, but peace of mind |

---

## What to Leave Behind

- Heavy luggage — you have one 7-seater for 7+ people on the northwest loop, space is genuinely tight
- Valuables beyond what's needed — Chaka, Qilian, Dachaidan are remote with limited security
- White or pale clothing — Danxia and desert dust will stain it immediately
- Too many formal clothes — nowhere on this trip requires anything beyond casual
