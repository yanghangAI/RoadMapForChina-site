## Overview

Xi'an's city wall is one of the best-preserved ancient fortifications in China. Built during the Ming Dynasty (1370s), it forms a complete rectangle around the old city centre — 13.7km in circumference, 12 metres high, and wide enough on top to cycle comfortably. Where the Great Wall is a mountain hike, the Xi'an wall is an elevated urban ride with views over the old city on one side and the modern skyline on the other.

The wall has four main gates (South, North, East, West) and regular watchtowers along its length. The South Gate (永宁门, Yongning Gate) is the most popular entry point and the most photogenic, with a large plaza below. From the top, you get a panoramic perspective of Xi'an that you cannot get from street level — the grid layout of the old city, the Bell Tower in the centre, and the contrast between ancient and modern architecture.

Most visitors rent a bicycle and ride the full loop. At a comfortable pace, the circuit takes about 1.5–2 hours. It is flat, paved, and shaded in stretches. In July it will be hot, so bring water and consider going in the late afternoon when the sun is lower.

## What to See & Do

- **Rent a bike at the South Gate** — single bikes (¥45/3h) and tandems (¥90/3h) available. Deposit required.
- **Ride the full 13.7km loop** — flat and easy. Takes 1.5–2 hours at a relaxed pace with photo stops.
- **Watch for the corner towers** — the four corners have the best vantage points for photography.
- **Walk a shorter section if cycling isn't for everyone** — the South Gate to East Gate stretch (~3km) is the most scenic on foot.

## Best Time to Visit

Late afternoon (4–6pm) is ideal in summer — the light is softer, the temperature drops, and sunset from the wall is excellent. Avoid midday in July when the exposed ramparts get very hot with no shade.

## Practical Tips

- **Tickets:** ¥54/person. Buy at the gate or online.
- **Duration:** 1.5–2 hours for the full cycling loop, 1 hour for a walking section.
- **Bike rental:** Available at all four main gates. Return the bike at any gate — you don't have to come back to where you started.
- **Hydration:** Bring water. There are a few drink vendors on the wall but they're spaced far apart.
- **Closing time:** The wall closes at 7pm (April–October). Last entry 6:30pm.

## Why It's Worth It

Cycling on top of a 650-year-old wall that encircles an entire city centre is an experience unique to Xi'an. It is active without being strenuous, visual without being a museum, and gives you a sense of the city's scale and layout that no single street-level sight can match.
