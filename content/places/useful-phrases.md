# Useful Chinese Phrases · For Italian First-Timers

Pronunciation guide: **ǐ** = short "ee", **ü** = like French "u", **zh** = like "j" in "judge", **x** = like "sh" but softer, **q** = like "ch" but lighter, **c** = like "ts"

The 2 Chinese members will handle most communication. These phrases are for when you're separated, ordering food independently, or just want to connect with locals.

---

## Essentials

| Situation | Chinese | Pinyin | Sounds like |
|-----------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Thank you | 谢谢 | Xiè xiè | "Shyeh shyeh" |
| Hello | 你好 | Nǐ hǎo | "Nee how" |
| Excuse me / Sorry | 不好意思 | Bù hǎo yì si | "Boo how ee suh" |
| Yes / Correct | 对 | Duì | "Dway" |
| No / Not right | 不对 | Bù duì | "Boo dway" |
| I don't understand | 我听不懂 | Wǒ tīng bù dǒng | "Woh ting boo dong" |
| Do you speak English? | 你会说英语吗？ | Nǐ huì shuō Yīngyǔ ma? | "Nee hway shwoh Ying-yü ma" |
| Please speak slowly | 请说慢一点 | Qǐng shuō màn yīdiǎn | "Ching shwoh man ee-dyen" |

## Ordering Food

| Situation | Chinese | Pinyin |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| One portion of this (point at menu) | 这个一份 | Zhège yī fèn |
| This is delicious | 很好吃 | Hěn hǎo chī |
| Too spicy | 太辣了 | Tài là le |
| No coriander (cilantro) | 不要香菜 | Bù yào xiāngcài |
| Water / Cold water | 水 / 凉水 | Shuǐ / Liáng shuǐ |
| Beer | 啤酒 | Píjiǔ |
| The bill please | 买单 | Mǎi dān |
| How much? | 多少钱？ | Duōshǎo qián? |
