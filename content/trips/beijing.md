## Practical Notes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Weather** | July: 30–36°C, humid, afternoon thunderstorms possible. Sunscreen + light rain jacket essential. |
| **Driving** | Foreign licenses not valid — only your Chinese friend can drive. Central Beijing has paid lots at all sights (¥30–50/stop). |
| **Payment** | WeChat Pay / Alipay everywhere. Set up one Chinese friend's account for group payments. Cards rarely accepted. |
| **Language** | Your Chinese friend handles communication. Almost no English outside tourist sites. |
| **Great Wall** | Arrive Mutianyu by 9am to beat tour buses arriving 10–11am. |
| **Forbidden City** | No large bags inside. Photography allowed in courtyards, not inside halls. Tickets are timed entry — arrive punctually. |
| **Night train** | 2 soft sleeper compartments (4 berths each) = 6 berths total. Each compartment has a lockable door. Bring earplugs. |
